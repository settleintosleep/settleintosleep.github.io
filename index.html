Settle Into Sleep

A quiet place to slow down at night

Settle Into Sleep is a YouTube channel of long, calm sessions: a new original piano composition paired with a gentle soundscape (rain on the window, distant waves, a crackling fire) and a night scene made for each video.

Watch on YouTube: youtube.com/@SettleIntoSleep

Settle Into Sleep Uploader

Settle Into Sleep Uploader is a private tool used only by the owner of the Settle Into Sleep channel. It runs on the owner's own computer and uses the YouTube API Services to upload the channel's own videos, set their titles and descriptions, schedule their publication and schedule the channel's own live streams.

It is not offered to the public, it does not access any other person's account, and it does not collect data from viewers.
