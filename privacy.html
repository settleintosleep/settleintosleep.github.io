Privacy Policy

Settle Into Sleep Uploader · Last updated: November 14, 2026

Who we are

Settle Into Sleep Uploader ("the app") is a private tool operated by the owner of the YouTube channel Settle Into Sleep. It is used only to manage that channel. Contact: settleintosleep@gmail.com.

YouTube API Services

The app uses the YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What information the app accesses

  • Through Google sign-in (OAuth), the channel owner grants the app permission to manage the Settle Into Sleep YouTube account. The app uses this permission only to: read the channel's own identity (channel ID and title) to confirm it is the right channel; upload the channel's own videos; set their titles, descriptions, tags and privacy/schedule; and create and manage the channel's own live broadcasts.
  • The app does not access, collect or store information about viewers, subscribers, commenters or any other YouTube user, and it does not access any account other than the channel owner's.

How information is stored

  • The OAuth refresh token is stored only on the channel owner's own computer, in the Windows Credential Manager. It is never written to project files, logs or any server operated by us.
  • Records of the channel's own uploads (video ID, upload time, upload speed) are kept on the same computer for operating the channel.

Sharing

The app does not sell, rent, share or transfer any information to third parties. Data is sent only to Google/YouTube, as required to upload and manage the channel's own content. The app does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers, and this website does not collect personal information.

Use of Google user data

The app's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is not used for advertising or to train artificial intelligence models.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page: security.google.com/settings/security/permissions. When access is revoked, the stored token stops working and is deleted from the computer. Requests about data can be sent to settleintosleep@gmail.com and will be answered within 7 days.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date.
